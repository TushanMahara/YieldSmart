Complete technical breakdown of the trained scikit-learn models, data preprocessing pipeline, and validation benchmarks.

1. Crop Yield Model (Random Forest Regressor)

End-to-end scikit-learn Pipeline with ColumnTransformer:

Pipeline(steps=[ ('preprocessor', ColumnTransformer([ ('num', StandardScaler(), ['Temperature', 'Humidity', 'Wind_Speed', 'N', 'P', 'K']), ('cat', OneHotEncoder(), ['Crop_Type', 'Soil_Type']) ])), ('regressor', RandomForestRegressor(n_estimators=100, random_state=42)) ]) • Features: 8 (6 numeric, 2 categorical) • Target: Crop_Yield (Continuous kg/ha) • Evaluation R²: 0.9745 | RMSE: 4.1266 kg/ha

2. N-P-K Recommender (Multi-Output Regressor)

Reverse multi-target regression pipeline:

Pipeline(steps=[ ('preprocessor', ColumnTransformer([ ('num', StandardScaler(), ['Temperature', 'Humidity', 'Wind_Speed', 'Crop_Yield']), ('cat', OneHotEncoder(), ['Crop_Type', 'Soil_Type']) ])), ('regressor', MultiOutputRegressor(RandomForestRegressor(n_estimators=100))) ]) • Features: 6 (Crop_Type, Soil_Type, Weather, Target Yield) • Targets: [Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K)] • Overall Multi-Target R²: 1.0000
